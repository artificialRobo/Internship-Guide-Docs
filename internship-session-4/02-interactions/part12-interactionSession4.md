## 2. Project Overview: Hostel Food Feedback System

### Objective

* Swati developed a feedback and complaint management system for improving hostel food quality.
* The system allows students to provide feedback about:

  * Food taste.
  * Menu.
  * Food quality.
  * Hygiene issues.
  * Complaints and suggestions.

### Platform Development

The project was developed in multiple formats:

* Online form-based system.
* Website application.
* Mobile application.

---

## 3. Key Features of the System

### Student Feedback System

* Students can submit daily feedback.
* Feedback can be given at different times:

  * Morning.
  * Afternoon.
  * Evening.
  * Other meal times.

### Complaint Box

* Students can submit complaints regarding food-related issues.
* Helps management understand student concerns.

### Food Management Alerts

* The system includes an alert feature.
* It notifies food management when food stock is low.
* Helps improve food availability and planning.

---

## 4. Problem Being Addressed

The project aims to solve issues related to hostel food management:

* Poor food quality in some hostels.
* Lack of proper hygiene practices.
* Difficulty in collecting student opinions.
* Lack of an organized complaint system.

The goal is to create a system where student feedback can directly help improve food services.
