# Internship Discussion - 07: Doing 2 Internships at the same time?

## 1. Choosing a PGCI(L) Internship Project

**Available project options:**

* Overview of Power Grid
* Protection of Power Grid
* Transformers
* Isolators
* CT/PT (Current Transformer/Potential Transformer)
* Insulators

**Student’s concern:**

* Which topic would be most beneficial for interviews and future career?
* How to convert the internship into a strong electrical engineering profile?

---

## 2. Professor’s Recommendation: Choose Transformers

* Since the student already finds **transformers interesting** and has studied them in Electrical Machines, it matches his **core competency**.
* Transformers are a large and evolving field with many current challenges.
* Recommended approach:

  * Select **Transformer-related project**.
  * Study not only textbook concepts but also modern developments.
  * Use resources like ChatGPT and online research to understand:

    * Latest transformer technologies
    * Problems faced by transformers today
    * Failure mechanisms
    * Improvements being developed
    * Applications in power grids

**Key idea:**

> A project becomes valuable when you deeply understand the problems, current developments, and possible solutions—not just the theory.

---

## 3. How to Make the Internship Interview-Relevant

To stand out in interviews:

* Do not only explain what the equipment is.
* Understand:

  * What problems occur?
  * Why do failures happen?
  * How can failures be predicted or prevented?
  * What improvements can be made?

Example:

* For transformers:

  * Study transformer failures.
  * Explore monitoring methods.
  * Analyze data such as voltage spikes, temperature, or other parameters.
  * Suggest ways to improve reliability.

---

## 4. Handling Two Internships at the Same Time

**Student’s concern:**

* If two internships overlap, should one be hidden from companies?

**Professor’s response:**

* Do not hide information.
* It is not practical or ethical to claim two full-time internships at the same time.
* A person cannot physically work two 8 AM–5 PM internships simultaneously.

**Possible solution:**

* Request one organization for a flexible schedule:

  * Part-time work
  * Evening hours (example: 6 PM–10 PM)
* Explain the value you can bring to the organization.

---

## 5. Importance of Specialization

* Choose one area of interest and develop deep knowledge.
* Becoming specialized in one field can lead to success.
* For example:

  * If interested in transformers, learn everything about transformers:

    * Basic concepts
    * Practical problems
    * Industry challenges
    * Latest innovations

**Deep knowledge creates expertise.**

---

## 6. Research Approach for Any Project

Before presenting a project:

1. Collect information from multiple sources.
2. Understand:

   * Existing problems
   * Industry issues
   * Latest technological developments
   * Possible solutions
3. Develop your own understanding and opinions.

**Result:**

* You will communicate more confidently.
* You will appear more knowledgeable during interviews and discussions.

---

## Important Lessons

* Select projects based on your interest and core strengths.
* Transformers are a strong choice because they connect theory with real-world power systems.
* A good engineer studies beyond textbooks.
* Avoid hiding experiences; communicate honestly and find practical solutions.
* Specialization + continuous learning creates career opportunities.
* Always understand the **problem first; solutions follow naturally.**
