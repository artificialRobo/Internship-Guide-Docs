# Zoom Class Guidelines

## 1. Maintain Class Discipline

* Do not disturb the class during the Zoom session.
* Stay attentive and focused throughout the lecture.

## 2. Asking Questions

* If you have a question:

  * Raise your hand, **or**
  * Type your question in the chat box.
* The professor will identify and recognize you before allowing you to unmute and ask your question.

## 3. Use the Chat Box

* Continue writing your questions in the chat as they arise.
* The chat helps organize questions without interrupting the lecture.

## 4. Stay Engaged

* Listen carefully to the discussion and explanations.
* Remain present in the class instead of leaving or getting distracted.

## In-short

Follow Zoom etiquette: don't interrupt, ask questions through the raise hand feature or chat, stay attentive, and actively participate in the class.
