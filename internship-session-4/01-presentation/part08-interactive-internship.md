# Interactive Internship Session

## 1. Purpose of the Interactive Session

* The session is designed for **students to speak more** and share their experiences.
* Faculty members aim to:

  * Listen to students' problems.
  * Provide appropriate guidance and solutions.
* Many students may face similar challenges, so discussing them benefits everyone.

## 2. Maintain a Daily Internship Notebook

* Keep a notebook or journal of your daily internship activities.
* Record:

  * Daily tasks completed.
  * Achievements.
  * Challenges faced.
  * New learnings.
  * Instructions given by your supervisor.

## 3. Why Daily Notes Are Important

* Help prepare weekly or periodic reports for your manager.
* Prevent forgetting important details.
* Keep track of work progress.
* Serve as evidence of your contributions.
* Become useful references during future job interviews.

## 4. Record Supervisor's Instructions

* Write down every task assigned by your supervisor.
* Inform your supervisor as soon as the assigned work is completed.
* This demonstrates professionalism and accountability.

## 5. Follow the SMART Approach

Tasks should be:

* **S** – Specific
* **M** – Measurable
* **A** – Achievable
* **R** – Relevant
* **T** – Time-bound

If a task is unclear, it is your responsibility to ask questions until you understand:

* What exactly needs to be done.
* How success will be measured.
* When it should be completed.

## 6. Performance Measurement

* Internship performance is evaluated over the internship period (e.g., 2–3 months).
* Keep evidence of your work through proper documentation.
* Measurable results are essential for evaluating your performance.

## 7. Reflect on Your Responsibilities

Your notes should include:

* Your assigned responsibilities.
* Data or information collected.
* Problems identified.
* Suggestions or solutions you proposed.
* Outcomes or improvements achieved.

## Quick Summary

Maintain detailed daily notes, communicate clearly with your supervisor, ensure every task is SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound), and regularly reflect on your responsibilities and contributions. Proper documentation is essential for performance evaluation and future career opportunities.
