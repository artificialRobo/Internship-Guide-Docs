# What you should do in an Internship

## 1. Document Every Process

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/document.png" alt="" width="auto">
</p>

* Keep all internship activities and processes properly documented.
* Documentation is an important professional skill and a key learning objective of the internship.

## 2. Research Before Joining an Internship

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/research.png" alt="" width="auto">
</p>

Before starting an internship, students should understand:

* The company's background and business.
* Its financial condition.
* Its major products/services.
* Which products are performing well and which are not.
* The purpose of the internship and expected learning outcomes.

> **Tip:** Use tools like ChatGPT to research the company before joining.

## 3. Student Success Builds College Reputation

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/student-success.png" alt="" width="auto">
</p>

* Good performance during internships reflects positively on the college.
* A strong impression on supervisors can:

  * Increase employment opportunities for current students.
  * Help future students secure internships in the same organizations.

## 4. Faculty–Industry Interaction

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/academic-industry.png" alt="" width="auto">
</p>

* Faculty members should regularly visit industries (e.g., at least one industry visit per week).
* These interactions help:

  * Build industry partnerships.
  * Create more internship opportunities for students.

## 5. Value Proposition of an Institute

* The true value of a college is measured by the employability of its graduates.
* The primary goal of higher education is to prepare students for successful careers.

## 6. Jharkhand Government Internship Stipend (2023 Policy)

* Eligible students who successfully complete their internships may receive a **₹100,000 (₹1 lakh) stipend**, provided they satisfy the prescribed eligibility conditions.

## 7. Changing Career Landscape

* Traditionally, parents and mentors encouraged students to pursue government jobs.
* However, the economy is shifting:

  * From a **knowledge-based economy** to a **curiosity- and creativity-driven economy**.
  * Repetitive jobs, including many routine government and corporate roles, are increasingly being automated.

## 8. Skills for the Future

Students should focus on developing:

* Curiosity
* Creativity
* Innovation
* Problem-solving abilities
* Adaptability to changing industry needs

## Quick Summary

A successful internship requires preparation, documentation, active learning, and continuous innovation. In today's evolving economy, creativity and curiosity are becoming more valuable than routine, repetitive skills, making employability the key outcome of higher education.
